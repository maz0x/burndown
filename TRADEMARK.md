# Name and brand

The Apache License 2.0 covers Burndown's **code**. It does not grant any rights
to its **name or brand**, and section 6 of the license says so explicitly.

To be concrete about what that means in practice:

**You may**, without asking:

- Use, modify, and redistribute the code, including commercially, under the
  terms of the Apache License 2.0.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Burndown", "a fork of
  Burndown", or "compatible with Burndown".
- Keep the name for an unmodified build you compiled yourself.

**Please do not**, without written permission:

- Publish a modified version under the name **Burndown**, or under a name
  close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the flame mark, the app icon, or the wordmark as the identity of your
  own product, app listing, or website.
- Present a fork in a way that suggests it is the official release, or that it
  is endorsed by or affiliated with this project.

The point is narrow. Fork it, sell it, build on it: that is what the license is
for. Just give your version its own name, so nobody downloads yours believing
it is this one.

Questions, or want permission for something not covered here? Open a
[discussion](https://github.com/maz0x/burndown/discussions).
